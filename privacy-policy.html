Privacy Policy

Trapped Arrow

Bulletplay Studios

Effective Date: October 4, 2026

Bulletplay Studios ("we", "us", or "our") operates the mobile game Trapped Arrow (the "Game").

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you download, install, or use Trapped Arrow.

By using the Game, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

Trapped Arrow does not require you to create an account or provide personal contact information.

The Game allows you to enter a player name and select an avatar. This information is stored locally on your device and is not submitted by the Game to a Bulletplay Studios server.

The Game does not directly ask you to provide:

  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Physical address
  • Contacts
  • Photographs
  • Camera access
  • Microphone access
  • GPS or precise location information
  • Payment card information

2. Locally Stored Game Data

Trapped Arrow stores certain gameplay and preference information locally on your device.

This may include:

  • Player name
  • Selected avatar
  • Current level
  • Completed levels
  • Coins
  • Game settings
  • Other gameplay progress and preferences

This information is used to save your game progress and provide the Game's functionality.

Bulletplay Studios does not operate a user account database for Trapped Arrow.

If you uninstall the Game or clear its application data, locally stored game information may be deleted.

3. Advertising

Trapped Arrow uses third-party advertising services provided through Unity Ads / Unity LevelPlay.

The Game may display:

  • Banner advertisements
  • Interstitial advertisements
  • Rewarded advertisements

Rewarded advertisements may require an internet connection. You can continue to play the Game without an internet connection, but certain rewarded advertising features may not be available.

Third-party advertising providers may collect or process technical information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, approximate location derived from network information, and information about advertising interactions, subject to their own privacy practices and applicable laws.

This information may be used for purposes including:

  • Delivering advertisements
  • Measuring advertising performance
  • Preventing advertising fraud
  • Limiting advertisement frequency
  • Personalizing advertisements where permitted
  • Improving advertising services

4. Analytics

Trapped Arrow uses Unity Analytics to understand how the Game is used and to improve gameplay, performance, stability, and the overall player experience.

Analytics information may include information such as:

  • Game events and interactions
  • Gameplay activity
  • Session information
  • Installation identifiers
  • Device and application information
  • Performance-related information

We do not use Unity Analytics to collect your name, email address, telephone number, contacts, photographs, camera recordings, or microphone recordings.

5. Crash Reporting and Diagnostics

Trapped Arrow uses Unity diagnostic services to help us identify crashes, errors, performance problems, and technical issues.

Diagnostic information may include:

  • Crash reports
  • Error information
  • Application performance information
  • Device and technical information
  • Installation-specific identifiers
  • Other information necessary to diagnose technical problems

This information helps us improve the stability and reliability of Trapped Arrow.

6. Internet Connection

Trapped Arrow does not require a permanent internet connection for normal gameplay.

An internet connection may be required for certain features, particularly advertisements and rewarded advertisements.

If you play offline, the core gameplay may continue to function, but advertisements or certain online-dependent features may not be available.

7. How We Use Information

Information handled by the Game and its service providers may be used to:

  • Save and restore gameplay progress
  • Provide Game functionality
  • Display advertisements
  • Provide rewarded advertisements
  • Measure advertising performance
  • Understand gameplay usage
  • Improve the Game
  • Detect and diagnose crashes and technical problems
  • Improve Game stability and performance

We do not sell your player name, avatar, or locally stored game progress.

8. Third-Party Services

Trapped Arrow uses third-party services, including Unity services, to provide advertising, analytics, and diagnostic functionality.

These third-party services may independently process certain technical information in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the Game.

However, no method of electronic transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Because your player name, avatar, coins, levels, settings, and gameplay progress are stored locally, you are responsible for protecting access to your device.

10. Data Retention

Locally stored gameplay information generally remains on your device until you delete the Game, clear its application data, or otherwise remove the stored information.

Information processed by third-party services such as Unity Analytics, advertising services, and diagnostic services may be retained according to the applicable policies and retention practices of those service providers.

11. Children's Privacy

Trapped Arrow is a general-audience puzzle game intended for users aged 13 and older.

The Game does not knowingly request a child's name, email address, telephone number, photographs, contacts, precise location, camera recordings, or microphone recordings.

Because the Game uses third-party advertising and analytics services, we take applicable children's privacy requirements seriously.

12. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights regarding personal information processed through third-party services, including rights to access, delete, correct, restrict, or object to certain processing.

Because some information is processed directly by third-party service providers such as Unity, requests relating to information held by those providers may need to be handled through the applicable provider's privacy mechanisms.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

When we make changes, we will update the "Effective Date" at the beginning of this Privacy Policy.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
